# Camp Placement Trade‑Off Explorer

This project is a visual tool to help Burning Man participants make **intentional trade‑offs** about where and how they camp.

At Burning Man, you rarely get everything you want at the same time.  
This tool makes one of the most important trade‑offs explicit: **visibility vs. quiet.**

---

## Core Idea

Camp placement exists along a single, unavoidable spectrum:

- **More visibility → more noise**
- **More quiet → fewer visitors**

There are no locations that provide both high visibility and low noise.

The goal of this tool is not to label placements as “good” or “bad”, but to help camps clearly understand **what they are choosing** and **what they are giving up**.

---

## Camp Placement View

The main view displays an **X–Y graph**:

- **X‑axis:** Visibility / foot traffic  
  (How many people are likely to encounter your camp)

- **Y‑axis:** Noise level  
  (How loud and active the surrounding area tends to be)

In practice, camps do not occupy all parts of the graph.  
Instead, they fall along a **diagonal trade‑off curve**:

- **Quiet, hidden camps** → easier sleep, fewer spontaneous visitors
- **Loud, visible camps** → more engagement, less rest

Increasing one inherently increases the other.

---

## Why This Exists

Many placement frustrations come from misaligned expectations:

- “Why is it so loud here?”
- “Why does no one ever find our camp?”
- “We wanted to interact, but also rest.”

This tool helps camps align:
- their **values**
- their **programming**
- their **placement expectations**

*before* they arrive on playa.

---

## How to Use It

1. Decide what your camp values more:
   - Sleep and quiet
   - Interaction and visibility
2. Locate yourself along the trade‑off curve
3. Design your camp layout, sound policy, and programming to match that reality

The tool does not recommend a “best” placement — only a **conscious one**.

---

## Additional Trade‑Off Tabs (Planned)

This project will expand to include other common Burning Man trade‑offs, presented in the same clear, expectation‑setting way.

Planned tabs include:

### 🔥 Effort vs. Impact
- High production value requires more build, strike, and maintenance
- Low effort preserves energy but limits scale and reach

### 💤 Participation vs. Burnout
- Saying “yes” to everything increases engagement
- Saying “no” preserves capacity and well‑being

### 🎁 Gifting vs. Logistics
- Bigger gifts create deeper connections
- Bigger gifts require storage, transport, and planning

### 🧭 Structure vs. Spontaneity
- Scheduling enables reliability
- Flexibility creates magic moments

Each tab highlights a **single unavoidable trade‑off** rather than idealized outcomes.

---

## Design Philosophy

- No optimization fantasies
- No “best of all worlds”
- No hidden assumptions

Just clear explanations of the choices we’re all already making.
